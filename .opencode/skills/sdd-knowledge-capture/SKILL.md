## What I do
- convert repeated findings into durable guidance
- tag entries for selective retrieval
- keep memory incremental without bloating active context

## When to use me
Use when a review or QA finding is likely to recur.

## Procedure
1. Distill the finding to a compact lesson.
2. Decide whether it is reviewer, implementer, or shared knowledge.
3. Create or update a short markdown entry.
4. Add a matching index entry in `.lla/manifests/knowledge-index.json` with tags and references.
